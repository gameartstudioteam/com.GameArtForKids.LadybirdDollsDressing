Privacy Policy

Ladybird Dolls Dressing

Last updated: October 3, 2026
Android app: com.GameArtForKids.LadybirdDollsDressing

This children's doll dressing game stores game progress and creations on the device. It uses Google AdMob for advertising with child-directed, non-personalized settings. Advertising services can process technical data even when personalized advertising and game analytics are disabled.

1. Developer and contact

GameArtForKids provides Ladybird Dolls Dressing. Parents, guardians and other users can send privacy questions or requests to gameartstudioteam@gmail.com.

2. Game information kept on your device

The game saves progress, earned game coins, unlocked items and language preferences locally. Saved outfits and colouring choices are stored as local game files. These records let you resume your game and keep your creations.

The game does not upload these saves to a developer-operated game server. It has no game account registration, chat, user upload service or developer cloud save account. Game coins are game rewards, not real-money purchases.

Where a game screenshot function is available, it saves an image of the game on the device. It does not photograph you or use the device camera to create that image. Images you separately copy, share or back up are subject to the services you choose.

3. Advertising and technical information

The game uses Google's AdMob advertising service for banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads. Google and its advertising service providers may process:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information, applicable device or other identifiers such as app set identifiers, and information about app launches, ad views, taps and other interactions;
  • diagnostics and performance information needed to operate and measure the advertising service.

These data support ad delivery, advertising measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Google processes them under Google's Privacy Policy. Its Mobile Ads data disclosure explains the service's data practices. Google's advertising SDK encrypts transmitted user data using TLS.

Child-directed treatment applies to every player. The game requests ads with a maximum content rating of G, disables personalized advertising and publisher first-party identification, does not request the Android advertising ID permission, and uses child-directed settings that disable advertising ID transmission. The game does not request precise location or ask children to enter their name, date of birth, address or contact details to play. Removing an advertising ID permission or disabling game analytics does not mean that the advertising service processes no identifiers. Technical data used by an advertising service are separate from local game saves.

Rewarded ads are optional and may provide game coins after an eligible ad is completed. The core game can be played without watching rewarded ads. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not turn off other advertising requests or their technical data processing. Internet access is needed for advertisements and external webpages.

4. Children and advertising privacy choices

The game is designed for children and uses child-directed advertising settings for all users. It does not ask a child to authorize personalized ads. Google's consent component is configured for users under the age of consent and controls whether ads may be requested. Applicable advertising privacy options, when required by that component, can be accessed through the game's privacy panel.

Parents and guardians may contact us about the game's privacy practices. Please avoid sending a child's personal details or saved files unless they are necessary to explain your request. Google's consent and child-directed settings do not replace any legal requirements that apply to the developer.

5. Game analytics

Firebase Analytics collection and Unity Analytics are disabled in this release configuration, as is Unity crash-report capture. Advertising diagnostics and measurement may still be processed by Google as described above; disabling game analytics does not disable all advertising data processing.

6. Privacy inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to and handle your inquiry. Correspondence is retained for as long as necessary for that purpose and any legitimate legal requirements. You may request deletion of correspondence by contacting the address above; any information that must be retained for legal reasons is handled accordingly.

Email is processed by the email providers involved in sending and receiving your message, under their privacy terms. The game itself does not collect an email address to let you play.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until it is overwritten through gameplay or removed using the device's app-data controls. The game does not have an automatic time-based expiry for local saves.

A parent, guardian or device owner can delete app-private game information using Android Settings, choosing this app and clearing its storage or app data. The exact menu name varies by device. Uninstalling removes app-private data from the device; progress, coins and saved outfits may be lost. Files saved outside app-private storage and operating-system or cloud backups must be managed separately. The developer cannot remotely clear files or backups on your device.

Google determines retention of data it processes for its services. See Google's data-retention explanation and the controls described in Google's Privacy Policy. Clearing this game's local data does not itself erase records already processed by Google.

The game has no developer game account to delete. Privacy questions and requests about correspondence or the game's data practices can be sent to gameartstudioteam@gmail.com.

8. Data handling and external services

Local game files rely on the device's app-storage protections and are not described as end-to-end encrypted storage. Protecting access to the device and managing its backups also protects those saves. Google's advertising SDK uses encrypted transmission as described above. No online service or storage system can provide an absolute guarantee against every risk.

Opening a Google privacy page, an advertisement destination or another external website takes you to that provider's service and privacy practices. Those services may handle additional information when you choose to interact with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game's data practices change. The last-updated date above identifies this version. Questions about an update can be sent to our privacy contact.
