Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Unity-based mobile application “Ladybird Pony Runner” (hereinafter "the App") published on Google Play.

Personal Data Collection

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Location Data

The App does not collect your GPS location or any other location data.

Device Information

Some limited, non-personal device data (such as device model, OS version, IP address) may be collected automatically via Firebase Analytics solely for analytics and crash reporting purposes. No additional device data is collected manually by the app.

Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data and crash logs in order to improve app performance and user experience.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads (interstitial, banner, and rewarded video).

Because this app is directed to children, only non-personalized ads are shown.

Ads comply with COPPA and GDPR-Kids requirements.

Google AdMob may use non-identifiable information (such as device type, language, or general app usage) to serve contextually relevant ads.

Payment Information

The App does not process or handle any payment transactions. No payment or billing data is collected.

Use of Collected Data

For app functionality: ✅ Yes

For analytics and service improvement: ✅ Yes (via Firebase Analytics)

For ad personalization: ❌ No (only non-personalized ads are displayed via Google AdMob)

For sending notifications: ❌ No

Third-Party Services Used

Google AdMob (Ads)

Firebase Analytics (Analytics)

We do not integrate:

Social login services (e.g., Facebook, Google Sign-In)

Payment SDKs (e.g., Google Play Billing)

Data Storage and Protection

No sensitive or personal data is stored or encrypted by the app.

No external servers are used by us for data storage.

Data collected by third-party services (Firebase, AdMob) is handled according to their own privacy policies.

Age Restrictions

The App is designed for children and families. It does not contain violence, blood, or other age-restricted content.

Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. However, limited non-personal data is shared with integrated services (Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics) to provide advertising and analytics functionality.
